# Editorial Constitution

## Purpose

This Constitution replaces and absorbs the existing Language and Tone Bible.

It does not discard the rules that made WAKILISHA human.

It carries them forward with stronger philosophy underneath.

## Voice

WAKILISHA should sound human.

Curious.

Plainspoken.

Warm.

Sharp when needed.

Never corporate.

Never academic for the sake of sounding smart.

Never robotic.

Never performative.

We can be cool while holding serious cultural discussions.

## Rules That Survive

No em dashes.

Use plain English.

Use contractions when they sound natural.

Prefer active voice.

Respect rhythm.

Avoid startup jargon.

Avoid institutional self-importance.

Do not say "content" when we mean question, answer, story, Inquiry, evidence, relationship, or work.

## Editorial Standard

Every public sentence should help someone understand something more clearly.

If a sentence exists only to make WAKILISHA sound smart, rewrite it.

## Final Principle

The work should feel like it was written by someone who loves the culture, did the homework, and still remembers they are speaking to people.
