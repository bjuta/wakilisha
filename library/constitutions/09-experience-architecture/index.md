# Experience Architecture

## Purpose

This replaces the idea of Information Architecture.

WAKILISHA is not only organizing pages.

It is organizing curiosity.

## Human Behavior

The behavior we want is:

Curiosity

Understanding

Curiosity

Understanding

Connection

Perspective

A visitor should leave more curious than when they arrived.

## Pages Are Stepping Stones

Artist pages are stepping stones.

Track pages are stepping stones.

Articles are stepping stones.

Charts are stepping stones.

Magazine issues are stepping stones.

The question is not whether a page exists.

The question is whether the page creates understanding and opens better doors.

## Every Surface Has A Job

Website creates understanding.

Socials reinforce identity.

Email continues correspondence.

Events answer questions that require people to gather.

Search lets people enter the network from anywhere.

## Final Principle

Every experience should answer one question while creating three better ones.
