# The Sixty-Year Test

## The Question

If the Board of WAKILISHA asks us sixty years from now why we made this decision, will we still be proud of our answer?

## Use

Apply this test to foundational decisions.

Names.

Institutions.

Rights.

Community.

AI.

Editorial principles.

Product direction.

Governance.

Do not use it to slow down small reversible decisions.

Use it when a decision shapes what WAKILISHA becomes.

## What Fails The Test

Fashionable decisions.

Trend-chasing decisions.

Decisions made only because competitors did them.

Decisions that trade trust for speed.

Decisions that make WAKILISHA bigger but less true.

## What Passes The Test

Decisions rooted in evidence.

Decisions that protect people.

Decisions that improve understanding.

Decisions that future Inquirers can amend without losing the institution.

## Final Principle

Products optimize for adoption.

Institutions optimize for endurance.

WAKILISHA must know which kind of decision it is making.
