# The North Star

## Mission

Help people understand African culture a little better today than they did yesterday.

## Long-Term Aspiration

Become the most trusted place to understand African culture.

Trust is earned. It is never declared.

If WAKILISHA does the work well, others may one day describe it as a trusted memory layer for African culture. We do not need to call ourselves that every day.

## What Business Are We In?

We are not in the streaming business.

We are not in the news business.

We are not in the social media business.

We are not in the encyclopedia business.

We are in the business of helping people understand African culture.

Music is where we begin.

It is not where we end.

## What We Build

Everything we build should do at least one of four things.

1. Increase understanding.
2. Increase trust.
3. Increase context.
4. Increase connection.

If a feature achieves none of these, it probably should not exist.

## What We Will Never Optimize For

We will never chase engagement at the expense of understanding.

We will never encourage performance instead of contribution.

We will never sacrifice trust for speed.

We will never build features simply because other platforms have them.

## Final Principle

Leave WAKILISHA more trustworthy than you found it.

Leave the cultural record clearer than you inherited it.
