# Community Constitution

## First Principle

People hold culture.

No editorial team can remember an entire continent.

Communities remember.

Families remember.

Artists remember.

Neighbours remember.

Diasporas remember.

WAKILISHA exists to gather these memories responsibly.

## Community Is Not Noise

Community does not exist to generate engagement.

Community exists to improve understanding.

The best contribution leaves the record clearer than before.

## Contribution Over Performance

People should not be invited to simply post culture.

They should be invited to help make the story clearer.

They may:

suggest corrections

add sources

share memories

identify missing context

clarify relationships

challenge assumptions

submit owned material

## Final Principle

The goal is not participation.

The goal is better answers.
