# Evidence and Truth

## Purpose

Trust is not a tone.

Trust is a practice.

This Constitution defines how WAKILISHA earns the right to be believed.

## Evidence

Claims should be supported where possible.

Sources matter.

Dates matter.

Attribution matters.

Corrections matter.

Uncertainty matters.

If sources disagree, we say so.

If we do not know, we say so.

If something is widely believed but not verified, we say so.

## Rights

Respect for creators is part of respecting culture.

Copyright and intellectual property are not legal afterthoughts.

They are part of the product.

If we cannot explain why we have the right to display, quote, store, distribute, or reuse something, we do not use it blindly.

## AI

AI may assist with research, organization, comparison, summarization, and pattern recognition.

AI must not invent certainty.

AI must not replace human judgment on significance, dispute, interpretation, or cultural meaning.

## Final Principle

The cultural record should become clearer because WAKILISHA touched it.
