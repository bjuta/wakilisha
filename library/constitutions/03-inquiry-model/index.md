# The Inquiry Model

## The Unit of Work

The fundamental unit of work at WAKILISHA is not content.

It is an Inquiry.

An Inquiry begins with one meaningful question and follows that question wherever the evidence honestly leads.

An Inquiry may last one week, three months, one year, five years, or an entire career.

There is no expectation that every Inquiry reaches a final conclusion.

Some become richer because the questions become better.

## The Inquiry Loop

Question

Investigation

Contribution

Relationships

Understanding

New Questions

Back to Investigation.

## The Inquirer

The person leading an Inquiry is an Inquirer.

Externally, some may be called Cultural Researchers.

Internally, Inquirer is the identity.

An Inquirer is not defined by profession.

An engineer can support an Inquiry.

A designer can reveal an Inquiry.

A contributor can strengthen an Inquiry.

AI can accelerate parts of an Inquiry.

Human judgment remains responsible for meaning.

## Law of Depth

WAKILISHA values depth over volume.

One Inquiry that reshapes understanding is more valuable than one hundred disconnected pieces of content.

## Final Principle

We are not building a library of finished work.

We are building a living institution of ongoing cultural inquiry.
