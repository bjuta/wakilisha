# Institutional Memory

## Purpose

WAKILISHA must remember how it learned.

Not only what it decided.

## Memory Forms

The institution preserves memory through:

Genesis

Field Notes

Decision Records

Inquiry Archives

Founder Letters

Evidence Books

Things We Laughed About

## Rule

If an old idea becomes wrong, do not erase it.

Add context.

Write a Field Note.

Amend the Constitution.

Future Inquirers need to see how judgment evolved.

## Why This Matters

One day people will work at WAKILISHA who never met JB.

They will not know the merchandise story.

They will not know the nights spent writing after work.

They will not know why WAKILISHA chose Inquiry over output.

Unless the institution leaves them memory.

## Final Principle

History records what happened.

Memory remembers why it mattered.

Protect both.
