# The Relationship Constitution

## First Principle

Relationships hold meaning.

A song by itself is a song.

A photograph by itself is a photograph.

An outfit by itself is clothing.

They become culturally meaningful because of the web of relationships around them.

## The Law of Earned Expansion

WAKILISHA does not pursue coverage.

It follows questions.

We do not decide to cover countries, genres, industries, or art forms because they are important.

We follow questions wherever they honestly lead.

If answering a question requires understanding Senegal, we go to Senegal.

If it requires understanding Jamaica, London, Haiti, Congo, Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa, China, or the diaspora, we go there too.

Our scope is determined by the relationships needed to tell the truth.

Not by geographic boundaries or editorial quotas.

## Music As Entry Network

Music remains our first domain because music naturally connects people, places, languages, politics, migration, fashion, film, religion, technology, and memory.

Music is not a cage.

It is an entry network.

## Final Principle

WAKILISHA expands only when a relationship is necessary to answer an important question truthfully.
