# Genesis Field Note

## Date

June 28, 2026

## What Changed

WAKILISHA stopped describing itself as a publishing platform and began describing itself as an institution for answering meaningful questions about African culture.

## The Turning Point

The sentence was:

> The question chooses the medium.

That sentence changed how we understood articles, timelines, artist pages, charts, podcasts, festivals, software, and community.

They are not separate strategies.

They are different ways to answer questions.

## Why It Matters

This gives WAKILISHA permission to expand without becoming unfocused.

We do not expand by coverage quota.

We expand when a question requires a relationship to be understood.

## New Questions

What kinds of questions has WAKILISHA earned the right to answer?

What should become an Inquiry?

How should future Inquirers inherit this way of thinking?
