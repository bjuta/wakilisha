Your people are here.
Their roots are on the record.

One system for WAKILISHA, the home for music and culture, and MIZIZI, the record underneath it. This workbook sets the foundations, tokens, components and patterns both share, and where they differ.

02 · Voice

One voice, two registers.

WAKILISHA is warm, editorial and confident. MIZIZI speaks the same way, then gets exact: it names sources, says what is known and what isn't, and never inflates a finding. Clear first. Warm always. Never generic.
