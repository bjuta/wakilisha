## Decision

MIZIZI is a shared cultural data intelligence and autonomous research system, not one independent LLM conversation per recording. Comprehensive real browser research is required. Bulk economics are a requirement, not permission to reduce capability or disguise unresolved cases as successes.

The unit of acquisition and analysis is the source or information gap: an artist catalogue, release, label, archive, publication, or particular recording as appropriate. The unit of a verified finding remains the individual subject and field. A shared source never merges identities or supplies independent corroboration of itself.

Use the existing Supabase, Registry, shared jobs, Headquarters, Decision Stream, financial and evidence custody infrastructure. Extend the existing Anthropic, native reader, PDF.js, Readability and Playwright implementations. No replacement agent framework, parallel queue, separate database, compulsory commercial search/browser service, or new subscription. Browser execution must be available through an independently qualified existing cloud allocation or compatible isolated Chromium host; do not assume either is already entitled or deployed. Use a provider-independent discovery interface; any search service is an adapter, not a release dependency. Respect source access, automated use and retention terms; keep credentials out of model context and webpages. Do not reduce legitimate network isolation, financial limits, or canonical authority. Do not require owner approval of every normal research action inside a bounded authorized session.
