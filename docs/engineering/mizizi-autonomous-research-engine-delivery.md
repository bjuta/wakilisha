## Decision

MIZIZI is a shared cultural data intelligence and autonomous research system, not one independent LLM conversation per recording. Comprehensive real browser research is required. Bulk economics are a requirement, not permission to reduce capability or disguise unresolved cases as successes.

The unit of acquisition and analysis is the source or information gap: an artist catalogue, release, label, archive, publication, or particular recording as appropriate. The unit of a verified finding remains the individual subject and field. A shared source never merges identities or supplies independent corroboration of itself.

Use the existing Supabase, Registry, shared jobs, Headquarters, Decision Stream, financial and evidence custody infrastructure. Extend the existing Anthropic, native reader, PDF.js, Readability and Playwright implementations. No replacement agent framework, parallel queue, separate database, compulsory commercial search/browser service, or new subscription. Browser execution must be available through an independently qualified existing cloud allocation or compatible isolated Chromium host; do not assume either is already entitled or deployed. Use a provider-independent discovery interface; any search service is an adapter, not a release dependency. Respect source access, automated use and retention terms; keep credentials out of model context and webpages. Do not reduce legitimate network isolation, financial limits, or canonical authority. Do not require owner approval of every normal research action inside a bounded authorized session.

Keep retrieval, interpretation and independent verification separate. Reuse source acquisitions, batch related requests and compact context; retain case/field-specific findings, exact evidence references and current permission checks. ### Work package 4: Real qualification

Benchmark a representative 1,000-recording cohort with independently reviewed ground truth and difficult cases: obscure African recordings, historical releases, incomplete metadata, conflicting credits, alternate versions, multilingual sources, dynamic sites and scanned material. Freeze cohort membership and adjudication rubric before execution. Report counts for attempted, accepted correct, accepted incorrect, unresolved/deferred, independently reviewed, unreviewable and exclusions. Do not silently discard expensive or difficult cases.

Proposed precision objective: >=99% among accepted resolutions, accompanied by actual coverage, uncertainty and reviewed sample size. Every accepted factual finding must be evidence-traceable. The inference engineering target is <=1,000 microusd per attempted recording on average ($0.001), not demonstrated; separately report all-in incremental cost and cost per independently confirmed correct resolution (including failed attempts, model calls, tools, browser runtime, hosting/storage allocation and retries). Report human review effort separately. No claim of target attainment from arithmetic, synthetic fixtures, selected successes, unmetered activity or unverified tariffs. A 1,000-recording cohort therefore has a $1 inference target, not an authorization to spend or a guaranteed outcome. If quality, coverage or economics miss, diagnose and improve; record genuine limitations instead of lowering the requirements silently.

Show real end-to-end research and reuse across subjects, autonomous continuation, browser interaction/visual evidence, and recovery from interruption, revocation, rate limits, unavailable/contradictory sources, budget exhaustion and attempted authority violations. Retain original/receipt integrity, independent finding verification and no unauthorized canonical mutation. Verify physical retention and backups handling under the actual deployed source-use policy; logical denial alone does not establish erasure. Slice 1 closes only on actual operational qualification, not synthetic replay or a native-only pass.
