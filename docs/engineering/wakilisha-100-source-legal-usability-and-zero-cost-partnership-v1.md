# WAKILISHA 100: Source-by-Source Legal Usability and Zero-Cost Data Partnership Assessment v1

**Research date:** 21 October 2026. **Case:** [WK100 issue #1264](https://github.com/bjuta/wakilisha/issues/1264). **Status:** legal/commercial desk-research finding, not an operational source admission, lawyer-signed opinion, provider agreement, or publication authorization. **Proposed source set:** existing five core research signals, plus two optional sources. **Admitted for automated WK100 collection: 0.**

## Executive determination

**An independently calculated multi-source Kenyan chart is legally possible in principle.** We have not found a general legal requirement that its publisher must obtain permission merely to **report individually observed, accurate, publicly available chart facts**. But the legality of (a) reporting a rank, (b) **systematically acquiring a full chart through a particular site or API**, (c) permanently archiving provider responses and (d) repurposing entire rankings in a recurring commercial composite are **separate** questions. A Kenya-law fair-dealing argument does not automatically defeat a distinct enforceable service contract or technical access restriction.

**No decision to replace the flagship with radio-only or listener voting.** Maintain independent source behavior classes and original methodology. An ordinal rank from Mdundo, an artist discovery rank from Shazam and a weekly YouTube view count are different evidence units; scoring cannot assert a Kenya-wide consumption rate where underlying data cannot substantiate one. This document narrows legal collection questions to exact endpoints and products rather than discarding measurement families.

**Commercial partnership finding:** There are documented data-reporting relationships within the chart industry, but **no verified precedent here for a zero-price WAKILISHA-style right to receive and republish a major platform's complete market-level stream ledger**. A **narrow, revocable, nonexclusive, aggregate/chart-rank pilot with attribution** is a plausible no-cash ask, not a confirmed offer or a statistically grounded probability. Distinguish platform *contributing* data to a compiler from compiler *obtaining* rights to resell or combine data.

## A. Governing tests, in required order

1. **Factual reporting:** Distinguish single verifiable source facts (song, ordinal rank, date, source) from copying a creatively selected compilation, branded pages, artworks, database, underlying media or substantially the same commercial chart. Accurate source credit is helpful but is not itself permission.
2. **Kenyan Copyright Act:** Read [Copyright Act, Cap. 130](https://copyright.go.ke/sites/default/files/downloads/CopyrightAct12of2001.pdf), in particular section 26(3) and the Second Schedule, Part A, for applicable fair dealing, quotation and acknowledgement exceptions. See the Supreme Court's [Communications Commission of Kenya v Royal Media Services, [2014] KESC 53 (KLR), paras 244–249](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kesc/2014/53/eng@2014-09-29), which applies purpose, character, amount, alternatives, nature and market-effect criteria. **This is a factual, purpose-and-amount test, not a blanket authorization to replicate complete Top 100 lists weekly.** The 2014 judgment quoted pre-amendment statutory wording; use current legislation for the actual exception.
3. **Contract and acquisition method:** Test formation/notice/acceptance, exact incorporated terms, entity operating the endpoint, host and access route, the relevant version and date, governing law, commercial use, storage/reuse and exclusions. A publicly viewable URL does not establish that a corporate collector has no applicable agreement. A public service's website clauses likewise are not by themselves a final judicial finding that all fact reporting is prohibited.
4. **Computer access:** No authentication bypass, proxy rotation to evade denials, CAPTCHA avoidance, geographic spoofing, rate-limit circumvention or use of undocumented private interfaces to defeat platform controls. Kenyan [Yegon v Republic [2024] KEHC 3128 (KLR)](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2024/3128/) records the elements of the section 14 security-measure offence. Not every contract disagreement is a section 14 offence, but this does **not** eliminate civil claims.
5. **Data treatment and measurement validity:** A chart fact's legal usability is not sufficient evidence of Kenya geographic scope, native chart period, official status or commercially permissible raw-byte retention. The producer's clock and WAKILISHA's Monday EAT observation week must be preserved separately. Repeated snapshots of the same unchanged top list are **not new listens**.
6. **Final operation gate:** Each exact source/method needs a dated, documented positive admission, approved manifest, native-period evidence and replay-safe receipt. This desk assessment **does not set any of those gates to PASS**.

Constitutional support: [Constitution of Kenya 2010](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2010/constitution/), Articles 11, 20, 33, 34, 35, 40 and 44 help frame independent cultural reporting and competing proprietary interests. These rights do not automatically compel a private provider to offer an API or license data, and Article 35 has its own restrictions. Foreign cases and UK database regulations can be comparative only, with applicable jurisdiction and contract carefully established.

## B. Seven-source usability determinations

**Key:** `EDITORIAL FACTS` refers to individual lawfully obtained, accurately attributed observations in original reporting. `SYSTEMATIC FEED` refers to recurrent unattended collection and archival of provider lists. `COMPOSITE` refers to using a substantial provider dataset within WAKILISHA's original flagship score. None of these three headings silently authorizes the others.

### 1. Mdundo Kenya top charts (`mdundo_ke`)

- **Actual studied source:** `play.mdundo.com/top-charts/ke`, nominal Top 100, ordinal platform-consumption proxy, native aggregation interval UNKNOWN; historical 28/28 technical-soak success, not current authorization.
- **Editorial facts:** credible argument for limited, attributed reporting of individual public chart positions under Kenyan principles; exact substantial extraction still requires scrutiny.
- **Systematic feed:** **NOT CLEARED.** Mdundo [general user terms, effective 9 July 2026](https://mdundo.com/terms) govern service use and provide a personal noncommercial licence. The separate [content-provider terms, updated January 2026](https://mdundo.com/terms/content) prohibit scraping and automated scripts. Do not incorrectly assume that content-provider terms alone control the WK100 observer: establish entity, assent, scope and governing law under the general terms as well.
- **Composite:** no established permission for automated replication/archival; a genuine independent data feed or narrowly negotiated licence can cure the collection/retention route.
- **Ruling:** **reporting position arguable; automated input deferred.** Highest-value Kenya-first low-cost pilot **ask**, not a legal free-pass. Determine whether partner provides dated chart ranks rather than underlying private stream counts.

### 2. Shazam Kenya Top 200 (`shazam_ke`)

- **Actual studied source:** public `shazam.com/services/charts/csv/top-200/kenya`, ordinal *discovery/identification* rank, not sales or streaming. Historical technical durability 28/28; official commercial CSV reuse grant **not established**.
- **Editorial facts:** strong factual-reporting case for selected source-attributed discovery ranks. A public downloadable CSV suggests an intended machine-readable distribution form, **not automatically a commercial onward-reuse licence**.
- **Systematic feed:** **NOT CLEARED** for a WK100 unattended collector and durable private raw-CSV archive. Need actual operative terms, official documented export licence/route, native period and limits.
- **Evidence of partnership precedent:** [Apple's Shazam privacy disclosure](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/music-recognition/) describes sharing deidentified aggregate recognition information with partners, including reporting to labels, artists and **chart compilers**. This demonstrates that partner reporting to chart compilers is a real use category; it is not permission for WK100.
- **Composite:** preserve Shazam as **discovery component**, structurally separated from consumption; no conversion to fictitious streams.
- **Ruling:** **editorial facts arguable; recurring raw CSV on hold.** Prioritize a nonexclusive rank-only chart-compiler pilot request, with rights to derive an original index, 7-day retained proof and attribution.

### 3. Boomplay chart-list playlist (`boomplay_ke`)

- **Actual studied source:** public chart-context playlist, researched `www-isp.boomplay.com/playlists/EQFJCbNTS0vEbeOL9pQjOToi?from=charts`, ordinal rank, historical technical availability 28/28 with distinct response digests and prior variation.
- **Editorial facts:** limited public observation reporting arguable, subject to content and IP scope.
- **Systematic feed:** **NOT CLEARED**. No verified current terms granting this exact host/list automated retrieval, archiving or cross-platform scoring.
- **Composite:** separate threshold: is this an **official provider-measured Kenya chart** or a playlist/editorial curation? The query string alone is not country or chart provenance. The operator should be independently verified.
- **Ruling:** **conditional source; hold for chart officiality and contractual acquisition.** A free official KE chart-rank data extract could be useful; a non-official playlist cannot be promoted to a national-consumption signal merely because permission is secured.

### 4. Audiomack Kenya geo chart (`audiomack_ke`)

- **Actual studied source:** `audiomack.com/geo-charts/playlist/kenya`, ordinal geo chart, historically 28/28 technical response.
- **Editorial facts:** source-attributed limited individual rankings remain analytically distinct from reconstructing or copying the service.
- **Systematic feed:** **HIGH SPECIFIC CONTRACT RISK; NOT CLEARED.** [Audiomack Terms of Service, Acceptable Use](https://audiomack.com/about/terms-of-service) expressly prohibit spiders/readers or similar tools retrieving or indexing any portion of the service outside the search-engine exception. The exact corporate/contractual applicability is fact-specific but cannot be presumed away.
- **Composite:** could be supported by a separate permitted export, licensed partner feed, or provider-provided chart aggregate, subject to exact rights. Do not recast manually mirroring complete lists as a term-free technical substitute.
- **Ruling:** **retain metric family and research, remove current web-automation method from launch-critical path.** Ask for a narrow nonexclusive partner deliverable if feasible; no claim that such a licence is already offered.

### 5. YouTube Kenya weekly Tracks Charts (`youtube_ke`)

- **Actual studied source:** `charts.youtube.com/youtubei/v1/browse`, weekly TRACKS/KE, ordinal rankings plus `viewCount`. This is **not** a documented YouTube Data API endpoint; provider week/finalization not yet proven.
- **Editorial facts:** factual observations can be reported in editorial context; beware reproducing the provider's entire ranking or false claims about what views measure.
- **Systematic feed:** **NOT CLEARED**. [YouTube Developer Policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies) prohibit scraping and place strict conditions on aggregation/combination and metrics. [The official policy guide](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies-guide) states restrictions on merging YouTube API data with other sources into derived metrics. Obtaining a quota ([default 10,000 units/day](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/guides/quota_and_compliance_audits)) is **not** a licence to use data in an unrestricted cross-provider WK100 index.
- **Composite:** **high-risk absent specific approved terms** for the chart use case, especially using view counts as an input into another provider-combined published rank.
- **Ruling:** preserve video-measurement dimension but replace undocumented endpoint reliance with a separately approved chart partnership/API use case, or independent licensed video-activity reporting. Do not turn generic channel-owner Analytics credentials into country-wide market data.

### 6. Apple public most-played, supplementary (`apple_ke`)

- **Actual studied source:** historic RSS `rss.applemarketingtools.com/api/v2/ke/music/most-played/100/songs.json`, ordinal ranks; 12/28 September success, remaining timeouts. Native period unknown.
- **Significant alternate official route:** Apple documents [Get Catalog Charts](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/applemusicapi/charts) with `GET /v1/catalog/{storefront}/charts`, `types=songs`, `chart=most-played`, and up to **200 entries**. A trusted [developer token](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/AppleMusicAPI/generating-developer-tokens) and developer programme membership are required. This makes a more credible technical acquisition surface than relying on an unstable public RSS endpoint.
- **Critical terms separation:** an API endpoint offering charts **does not automatically license independent commercial aggregation**. [Apple Music for Artists analytics terms](https://artists.apple.com/read_terms) restrict aggregation of its **private Content Analytics Service** with other content providers; this must **not** be confused with a general decision about the separate public Catalog Charts API, for which exact applicable licence/use terms must be checked.
- **Ruling:** **technical route upgrade candidate, legal composite use not yet cleared**. Retain supplementary classification until market, period, rights, and real availability are confirmed by an independently authorized bounded test.

### 7. Spotify public UGC playlist panel, optional

- **Actual studied source:** fixed 20 independently curated playlists, 556/560 prior observations; playlist position is not Spotify national consumption and cannot fill missing core country measurements.
- **Editorial facts:** individual playlist-position references possible subject to acquisition and limited quotation context.
- **Systematic feed/composite:** [Spotify Developer Policy, effective May 2025](https://developer.spotify.com/policy), section III, restricts derivative listenership metrics, benchmarking and analysis of Spotify Content/Service, while other provisions restrict commercial purposes and third-party integrations. A public user playlist is not automatically unrestricted analytic feed authority.
- **Ruling:** **optional, not core**. Retain as clearly labelled user-curation context only after identifying a permitted data path; do not use a Spotify developer token to create an unapproved national-popularity metric.

## C. What zero-cost partnerships can and cannot realistically mean

**Established precedent for data reporting, not verified zero-price rights for WK100:**

- [Luminate solicits registered streaming/sales providers](https://support.luminatedata.com/portal/en/kb/articles/become-a-data-provider), verifies minimum quality, and executes data-provider agreements. [Its methodology](https://support.luminatedata.com/portal/en/kb/articles/methodology) describes direct partner reports feeding Billboard. The published materials **do not establish** that a third-party independent compiler receives the same datasets for free.
- The UK [Official Charts Company recruits qualifying chart-reporting retailers](https://www.officialcharts.com/pro/register-to-be-a-chart-reporting-retailer/). Its [B2B analytics products](https://www.officialcharts.com/our-business-services/b2b-data/) are explicitly subscription/licence products. Source reporting and commercial onward access are different rights.
- Apple's [Shazam data practices](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/music-recognition/) explicitly include reporting to external *chart compilers*. This is the clearest proof in our researched source set that chart-compiler access is a recognized partnership purpose, **without revealing price or contract scope**.
- Apple's public developer API and YouTube's default API quota illustrate that **some interfaces permit entry without a custom enterprise-data agreement**, but legitimate developer tokens and quotas do not grant every independent commercial derived-use right, and programme membership may have a cost.

**Commercial judgment, not evidence of promised acceptance:**

| Requested deliverable | Zero-cash prospect | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time-limited, rank-only, market-specific CSV or weekly snapshot, nonexclusive, credited, no personally identifiable data | **Plausible negotiating starting point** for platforms seeking market visibility; not a guaranteed free grant | No named provider offer yet |
| Repeatable rank/period feed with retrospective private raw evidence, audit and right to publish an original multi-source rank | **Negotiable but materially more demanding**; licence scope and retained evidence must be explicit | No named provider offer yet |
| Full market-level stream counts, raw user interactions, commercially reusable historical archive or exclusive feed | **Expect negotiated fees or refusal** as a commercial hypothesis; no verified pricing | No published free data licence identified |

Do not invent a percentage likelihood of acceptance. A small emerging chart publisher has less negotiating leverage than a widely relied-upon industry compiler, but may offer locally valuable attribution, market discovery, artist visibility and nonexclusive credit.

**Narrow 90-day pilot ask for a provider, not an action authorized today:** no money; named Kenya product; stable export containing `source_key`, chart `market`, source-native start/end or as-of timestamp, `rank`, artist, title, stable provider recording ID where available; up to 100/200 ranks; weekly or native cadence; nonexclusive use in one independently computed WAKILISHA ranking; no raw customer data; private encrypted/hash-checked retention sufficient for independent replay; provider/source attribution; documented duration, opt-out and correction/dispute route; no implication of provider sponsorship; no entitlement to proprietary stream counts. Ask the provider to **confirm whether this narrow grant is available gratis** and, separately, quote a limited fee if not.

No contacts, emails, permission requests, or on-platform provider actions have been executed during this research.

## D. Fastest credible flagship route without sacrificing diversity

1. Preserve all **five independent original source hypotheses** and their source-specific metrics; do **not** silently change Edition 1 to radio-only, social voting, or a promotional playlist. Revisit any provider only at the acquisition level. An absent core source is not replaced by renormalizing its missing weight.
2. In parallel, select three **method-distinct lawful acquisition routes** for evaluation rather than calling five unsafe pages: (a) official Apple catalog chart API for supplementary consumption rank once terms permit; (b) Shazam's chart-compiler reporting partnership or expressly licensed CSV use as a discovery signal; (c) Mdundo permitted first-party export/licensed chart ranks as Kenya platform signal. Then seek Boomplay official Kenya market proof and an approved video source. These are **research priorities, not admitted sources or a claim that three sources automatically suffice**.
3. Before any flagship-scoring commitment, publish a *source coverage and sensitivity report*: at least three independent **operators** and at least two meaningfully distinct behavior families as a **proposed study threshold only**, not a preaccepted scoring constitution. Measure eligible tracks, duplicate recordings, KE market representativeness, depth, native periods, source disagreement and the top-100 impact when any single provider disappears. Approval depends on actual evidence; there is no automatic `N=3` go-live shortcut.
4. Complete bounded **prospective** acquisition using only independently admitted endpoints and recorded legal/retention authority, verified raw bytes, replayable receipts, transparent missingness and immutable blocked/nonpublishing freeze. The current `runCandidateCapture` supports precisely six keys (`mdundo_ke`, `audiomack_ke`, `boomplay_ke`, `shazam_ke`, `apple_ke`, `youtube_ke`). It has **no Spotify UGC collector**; a different approved Apple endpoint would require a reviewed source manifest/adapter acceptance rather than silent URL substitution. It currently returns `can_publish:false`.
5. A separate methodology and editor release gate determines whether the collected evidence is statistically, geographically and legally sufficient to present the flagship WAKILISHA 100 rather than an attributed *chart observation bulletin*. Independently corroborate the legal positions that still turn on enforceability, foreign law or substantially complete compilation reuse; a human lawyer validates narrow disputed conclusions, not the entire desk research.
