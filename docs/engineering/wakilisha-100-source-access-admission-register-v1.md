## Evidence precedence and status vocabulary

Sources of truth: [September 14 technical durability closure](charts-public-source-accessibility-soak-final.md), [WAKILISHA 100 Source Observation Contracts](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1fcPlSrOA-OCGfYoY2TQWLNlCyMOiA0orZX3zV7zcyh0/edit), [current integrated runtime authority](wakilisha-100-integrated-capture-runtime-v1.md), and provider's own terms. The live research spreadsheet documents candidate endpoints, native metric classes, source depth and unknowns; it is **not** an access grant. Terms below are research pointers, not legal advice or final contractual interpretation. Provider terms or arrangements may change.

- `BLOCKED — explicit automation / reuse concern`: an official public policy materially conflicts with the proposed collection method; require documented alternate authorized route or written provider permission.
- `HOLD — permission / market / period unverified`: available research cannot establish the exact rights and measurement contract; no provider fetch.
- `SUPPLEMENTARY HOLD`: not required for a full week; still needs independent approval for any use.
- `ADMITTED`: never inferred from this register. Requires a dated, source-specific reviewed decision and all the acceptance fields below.

A successful HTTP 200, public visibility, prior seven-day soak, parseable response, or country-looking URL **does not grant** a licence to automate retrieval, archive responses, display a derived chart, or poll repeatedly. Past soak successes must not be treated as new observations or prospective WK100 evidence.

## Admission record required per exact source

A reviewer must create a **separate dated evidence record per exact endpoint and contract version**, with:

1. `source_key`, stable provider operator/legal entity, exact HTTPS host/path/query, GET method, official chart title, source version and market basis. Do not replace an exact authority decision with a generic website approval.
2. Source-specific **written permission/licence or demonstrably applicable express terms**, original URL/document date, authorized WAKILISHA entity/purpose, third-party restrictions, attribution requirements, independent chart/aggregation permission, raw-byte retention and audit/replay rights, redistribution/publication limits, expiry/review date, named reviewer and decision status.
3. Approved credential/API mode, only presence and secret *reference* (never token values), official developer integration requirements, approved egress/DNS hostnames, project-specific Preview/Production secret parity, and documented prohibition on redirects, host substitution and unauthorized scraping.
4. Source behavior/measurement definition (rank, discovery intent, videos/views, UGC position), native geographic basis, top-N/depth and validation tests, native publication and start/end periods, update clock and EAT schedule, observed completeness/missingness, and retries that do not create new events.
5. Quota/rate/timeout and response-byte cap under the existing 4 MiB transport envelope, cache/retention and deletion requirements, 429/error handling, expected MIME/adapter SHA-256, evidence of sample payload validity acquired **only after authorization**, and expiry/revocation path.
6. Explicit `ADMIT / DENY / DEFER` decision, recorded approval artifact, reviewer role and date; another independent operator checks policy, native periods, endpoint and provider legitimacy. Any change of host, method, licence or native interval invalidates the previous admission until reviewed again.

Before any call, the runtime requires the separate independently SHA-256-sealed server-only `WK100_CAPTURE_SOURCE_MANIFEST` plus `WK100_CAPTURE_SOURCE_MANIFEST_SHA256`, project-bound `WK100_CAPTURE_PROJECT_REF` and separately approved `WK100_CAPTURE_ENABLED=true`. Its manifest entries must agree with a reviewed contract version, `source_key`, exact `endpoint`, `market=KE`, `method=GET`, `purpose=candidate_raw_evidence`, a nonempty `authorization_ref`, an unexpired `authorization_expires_at` and `enabled=true`. **Do not write these values, a credential or a synthetic admission state merely to pass the parser.**
