# WAKILISHA Magazine — Issue 002
## Art Direction Brief

**Art Director:** Muiruri Beautah  
**Creative School:** Swiss / International Typographic Style  
**Mode:** Light  
**Season:** Second issue  

---

## Creative Direction

Pure restraint. Issue 002 is where we demonstrate that Wakilisha can work without atmosphere. No radial glow, no dark dramatic palette. Just ink on cream. 

The Swiss commitment here is not nostalgia — it is an argument. Hierarchy through position, not decoration. The grid is decided first. Everything else follows from it.

One family. One accent. Black/white/red. Three values, infinite combinations.

---

## Typography

- **Display:** Inter — Light 300 for display, Black 900 for emphasis
- **Body:** Inter — Regular 400 for body, no serif in this issue
- **Mono:** DM Mono — for all metadata, section labels, coordinates
- **Rules:** Flush-left, ragged right. No centred compositions except by deliberate exception
- **Italic:** Only for citations, titles of works, and foreign words. Never for emphasis.
- **Weight contrast carries hierarchy.** If you want emphasis, go heavier, not italic.

---

## Colour

Cream background (#F5F3EE). Single accent: #D41C14 (Swiss red). Used only at critical moments — the first letter of the cover title, a column rule between sections, a folio number.

If a spread doesn't need the accent, it doesn't get it. Restraint is the argument.

---

## Composition Rules for This Issue

1. All content anchors to the grid. If it's floating, question it.
2. Asymmetric balance. Symmetric only as deliberate exception.
3. No borders, ornamental dividers, or decorative frames.
4. Photography treated as documentary evidence, not atmosphere.
5. Whitespace is not empty. It is composed.

---

## Spreads to Include

1. **Cover** — Type-only. Title at full bleed typographic scale. No photography.
2. **Grid Manifesto** — A spread that makes the grid visible as structure
3. **Editor's Note** — Single column, flush-left. Minimal.
4. **Table of Contents** — Clean, numbered, rule-separated
5. **Feature** — Two-column body, photography as grid module (not bleed)
6. **Section openers** — Number at ghost scale, section name flush-left
7. **Signal** — Data-driven. Numbers as first-class typography.
8. **Back matter** — One rule. Issue stats. Nothing else.

---

## Voice Notes

Swiss doesn't mean cold. It means precise. The writing in this issue should match the design: clear sentence structures, active voice, no ornamental language.

Pull quotes in this issue carry weight not from italic or colour, but from their size and position on the grid.

The cover title should feel like a poster in a transit corridor. You should be able to read it from 10 metres.

---

## What Makes This Issue Different From Issue 001

Issue 001 was warm, authorial, photographic. Issue 002 is disciplined, structural, evidential. Different magazine. Same publication. Same Wakilisha.

That's the point. No two issues look the same.
