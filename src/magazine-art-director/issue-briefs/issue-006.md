# WAKILISHA Magazine — Issue 006
## Art Direction Brief

**Art Director:** Muiruri Beautah  
**Creative School:** Japanese Minimal × Luxury Fashion Editorial  
**Mode:** Light  
**Season:** Sixth issue — the quiet one  

---

## Creative Direction

Emptiness is not absence. It is the primary field of attention.

Issue 006 asks the reader to slow down. The design should make the reader slower, not louder. Every spread has more space than content. That space is doing work.

This is the issue where the grid whispers instead of declares. Where a single photograph on an otherwise empty page is enough. Where *ma* — the Japanese concept of meaningful negative space — shapes every decision.

---

## Typography

- **Display:** Noto Sans — Light 300, generous tracking (0.08em)
- **Body:** Noto Sans — Regular 400, generous line height (1.8–2.0)
- **Mono:** DM Mono — minimal, only for absolutely necessary metadata
- **Rule:** Type is modest, precise, lightly held. No decorative effects.
- **Horizontal rules:** Used as breath markers between sections. Thin, full-width.

---

## Colour

Warm natural whites (#F8F6F2). Muted sage green accent (#5C7A5C). The green is almost grey. The background is almost paper. Almost.

There are no accent moments in this issue that shout. The accent appears at the folio, the section label, the first letter of the editor's note. That's it. Everywhere else is composed restraint.

---

## Spatial Principles

1. Every spread has at least 40% whitespace by area
2. Photographs are placed off-centre, aligned to the grid asymmetrically
3. No text is crowded against an edge without breathing room
4. Section openers have a full half-page of empty space

---

## Photography Direction

Photography in this issue should feel like a still frame: a single held moment. Not dynamic, not theatrical. The subject doing something ordinary. The light coming from one direction. The rest of the frame is rest.

Crop at the intimate edge: close enough to feel personal, far enough to feel quiet.

---

## Spreads to Include

1. **Cover** — Vast whitespace. Title in the lower left third. Noto Sans Light.
2. **Editor's Note** — The note should feel like a letter found in a book
3. **Contents** — Minimal index. Page numbers as small numerals.
4. **Feature** — Full-bleed photograph. Body text in one narrow column.
5. **Photo Essay** — Two composed images. One caption per image, max 12 words.
6. **Article lists** — Wide cards, generous padding, minimal text
7. **Back matter** — One line. The seal, small. One word from the issue.

---

## Voice Notes

Writing for this issue should have the pace of a person thinking out loud in a quiet room. Long sentences are fine. Let them breathe. Avoid urgency.

No sub-headlines. No bullet points. No call-out boxes. If the text needs those things to be understood, it hasn't been written well enough yet.

---

## The Japanese Minimal Test

Is there a single element on this spread that does not need to be here?  
If yes: remove it.  
If the spread feels "empty": do not add anything. Stay with the emptiness. It is doing work.
