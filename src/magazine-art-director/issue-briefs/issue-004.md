# WAKILISHA Magazine — Issue 004
## Art Direction Brief

**Art Director:** Muiruri Beautah  
**Creative School:** Bauhaus × Swiss  
**Mode:** Dark  
**Season:** Fourth issue  

---

## Creative Direction

Geometric primitives. Primary colour blocks. Diagonal energy.

Issue 004 reaches back to the Dessau building, to Bayer's Universal typeface, to the moment when the Bauhaus decided that form follows function and meant it. But we don't revive Bauhaus — we deploy it. The scaffold is Swiss order; the skin is Bauhaus chromatic energy.

Every composition can be diagrammed as circles, triangles, and rectangles. If it can't, something is wrong.

---

## Typography

- **Display:** Raleway — 300 light for titles, 900 black for counter-emphasis
- **Body:** Raleway — 400 regular
- **Mono:** DM Mono — for all metadata
- **Single-case where possible:** Uppercase for section openers, sentence case for body
- **Constructed letterform motifs:** Single letters used as visual objects in section openers

---

## Colour

Dark background (#060408). Red accent (#FF4040) — structural, not decorative. Blue secondary (#2860C0) for the cover geometric block. Yellow (#F5C800) used only for the geometric triangle device.

These three primaries — red, yellow, blue — appear as geometric forms in the cover and section openers. They are structural. They are not applied as decoration.

---

## Geometric Devices

The cover uses the Bauhaus triangle device: a red triangle occupying the bottom-right 38% of the viewport. A blue rectangle in the top-left corner. These are not background decorations — they are the composition.

Section openers use a single large letterform (the section's first letter) as a visual object, outlined in the section accent colour.

---

## Photography Direction

Photography in this issue is constructive, not documentary. Images are cropped into geometric modules, sometimes treated as duotone against a primary-colour field. A portrait cropped into a square and placed at 45 degrees is Bauhaus. A soft candid photo placed naturally is not.

## Voice Notes

The Bauhaus wrote manifestos. This issue should have a manifesto energy. The editor's note can open with a declaration. The section openers can name what the section *is for*, not what it contains.

"The Sound of Now" becomes "Sound is the loudest thing a culture can do."

The language is confident, pedagogical, building toward something.

---

## The Bauhaus Test

Is the composition asymmetric? If not, why not?  
Are the primary colours structural? If they feel decorative, remove them.  
Can the spread be diagrammed as geometric primitives? If not, simplify.
