# WAKILISHA Magazine — Issue 003
## Art Direction Brief

**Art Director:** Muiruri Beautah  
**Creative School:** Luxury Fashion Editorial × Editorial Magazine  
**Mode:** Light  
**Season:** Third issue  

---

## Creative Direction

The image is sovereign. This is the issue where we hand control to the photograph.

Everything in Issue 003 exists in service to the visual. Typography is placed like couture — precisely, rarely, controlled. A Didone headline at 120px italic over a full-bleed image is not decoration. It is the composition.

Whitespace signals price. A spread that has three words and one photograph is not empty. It is expensive.

---

## Typography

- **Display:** Cormorant Garamond — 300 weight, italic at hero scale
- **Body:** Cormorant Garamond — 400 regular, generous leading (1.7–1.9)
- **Mono:** DM Mono — whisper-thin, for credit lines and coordinates only
- **Caption style:** italic, 11pt, small caps for proper nouns
- **No sans-serif in this issue.** Serif only. The switch to sans would break the register.

---

## Colour

Warm cream (#FAF7F2). Gold accent (#C8A96E). This is not a bright gold — it is aged, amber-warm, the colour of a well-handled book.

No high contrast. Soft tonal layers throughout. Images carry the contrast; typography provides the quiet frame.

---

## Photography Direction

- Full-bleed mandatory on the cover and feature opener
- Crops can be severe: a hand, a jawline, a fragment of fabric
- No generic portraits. The crop must make a decision.
- Duotone treatment acceptable for pull sections
- Every image must feel authored, not found

---

## Spreads to Include

1. **Cover** — Full-bleed image, Cormorant Garamond title over the image in white at 120pt italic
2. **Editor's Note** — Sparse two-column. One image at left, flowing note at right.
3. **Contents** — Serif-only table, generous margin, editorial crop thumbnail for each section
4. **Feature** — Full-bleed opening, then body text across the fold
5. **Photo Essay** — Two images, composed together as a single event
6. **Article lists** — Each card gets a strong image and minimal text
7. **Back matter** — Single serif line on cream. The seal, small. That's it.

---

## Voice Notes

The voice of this issue is considered and unhurried. Sentences can be long. Paragraphs can breathe. The reader is not being rushed.

Pull quotes should carry emotional weight, not information. They should make you want to read the piece, not summarise it.

---

## The Luxury Test

Before placing any element on a spread: would this be at home in the pages of Self Service, System, or Purple? If not — if it feels crowded, cluttered, or approximate — take it off the page.

Luxury is not about looking expensive. It is about confidence that the reader's attention is worth the space.
