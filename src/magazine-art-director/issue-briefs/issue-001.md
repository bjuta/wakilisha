## Creative Direction

The launch issue. This is where we establish what Wakilisha Magazine *is* — not what it does. The visual language borrows from the Bloomberg Businessweek era under Richard Turley: Swiss compositional discipline as the skeleton, editorial warmth and photographic authorship as the skin.

Grid-first. But not cold. The grid here is a heartbeat, not a cage.

Every spread in this issue earns the next. Pacing is design. A dense, text-heavy feature should exhale into a quiet full-bleed portrait. A number-led signal spread should be followed by something handmade and warm.

---

## Typography

- **Display:** Playfair Display — italic for emphasis, upright for assertion
- **Body:** DM Serif Display — warm and readable at column width
- **Mono:** DM Mono — used for issue metadata, section labels, folios
- **Drop caps:** Mandatory on every feature opener. Large, accented.
- **Pull quotes:** 40pt italic Playfair, left-bordered with accent colour
- **Folio treatment:** Issue 001 · Nairobi · {section name}

---

## Colour

The palette here is warm dark: #0A0804 background, #C4883A accent. Not a design-system dark mode. A publishing dark mode. Ink on paper. Night session in a studio.

Accent used sparingly: drop caps, folio markers, the odd rule, the opening word of a section eyebrow.

When light surfaces appear (editor's note, table of contents), they should feel like turning to the inside of a jacket lining.

---

## Spreads to Include

1. **Cover** — Full-viewport, radial glow, Seal prominent, title breaking two lines
2. **Editor's Note** — Two-column: portrait side, flowing body copy, playlist if available
3. **Table of Contents** — The word "Contents" at 140px, then a clean index
4. **Feature** — Full-bleed opening image, deep typographic treatment on the copy
5. **Photo Essay** — Two strong images side by side, editorial captions
6. **Signal** — The cultural intelligence spread, bar chart as designed object
7. **Section openers** — One per section, number as ghost element
8. **Article lists** — Horizontal-scroll strips, one per section
9. **Back matter** — Seal, final line, stats, coordinates

---

## Voice Notes

The cover title for Issue 001 is "Your People Are Here." This should be treated as a declaration, not a headline. Set it broken across two lines: "Your people" / "are *here.*" The italic on "here" carries everything.

The editor's note should not sound like a launch press release. It should sound like a letter from someone who has been watching and finally decided to speak.

---

## Images

Photography should feel documentary, not staged. Faces in motion, rooms that have been used, instruments played, spaces occupied. No stock imagery. No perfect light.

For sections without photography: use full typographic spreads. The words should be large enough to feel like a room.

---

## What This Issue Is Not

- Not a blog with a dark background
- Not a streaming platform's editorial section
- Not a "premium" content product
- Not a document

This issue is a *record*. It exists after the event. It's the file, the archive, the thing you come back to.
