# WAKILISHA Magazine — Issue 010
## Art Direction Brief

**Art Director:** Muiruri Beautah  
**Creative School:** Brutalist Web × Memphis Postmodern  
**Mode:** Dark  
**Season:** Tenth issue — the confrontational one  

---

## Creative Direction

Structure is allowed to show itself. Polish is not proof of quality.

Issue 010 is the issue where we break our own conventions. After nine issues of increasing craft and intention, this one admits the machine. It shows the grid. It uses default-like elements as materials. The friction is intentional.

This is not anti-design. This is design that refuses to hide its thinking. The Brutalist Web tradition: the interface is the argument, the system is the content.

Memphis enters here as sabotage: colour clashes that short-circuit the brutalist severity. Neon yellow on concrete grey. Hot pink on black. A stage set assembled from raw materials.

---

## Typography

- **Display:** Space Grotesk — 900 weight, uppercase, all the way
- **Body:** Space Grotesk — Regular 400, but also Space Mono for some body sections
- **Mono:** Space Mono — mandatory for data, headers, navigation
- **Rules:** Uppercase is permitted and sometimes required in this issue
- **Visible links:** Underlined text behaves like browser defaults. Intentionally.

---

## Colour

Black background (#060606). Yellow accent (#F5E642) — neon, aggressive, honest.  
Hot pink secondary (#FF1493) for contrast moments.  
White for everything else.

No gradients. No soft transitions. Hard cuts between colour fields only.

---

## Grid as Content

Unlike every other issue: the grid should be slightly visible. A 2px line every 80px creates a background grid that says "you can see how this was built." This is Brutalist Web's first principle made visible.

---

## Borders

Thick borders are acceptable in this issue. A 2px solid border around a spread, a card, an article card. The border that Swiss design refuses — here it is a declaration.

---

1. **Cover** — Title in Space Grotesk 900, uppercase, broken across lines. Yellow accent border.

---

## Voice Notes

This issue's editorial voice is direct to the point of bluntness. No softening language. No hedging. "This is what happened. These are the numbers. This is what it means."

The editor's note should open by refusing the conventional format. "This is not a note. This is a record. Here is what we found."

---

## The Brutalist Test

Is the structure visible or hidden?  
If hidden: should it be?  
Is the friction intentional and expressive?  
Or is it just rough?  

If the structure is hidden and the friction is accidental, it's not brutalist — it's just unfinished. Brutalist Web is a commitment, not an excuse.
