## Creative Direction

Good taste is not a law.

Issue 005 is the issue where the magazine is allowed to have fun. Memphis colour clashes are the point, not an accident. Pattern is content. The stage set can feel like a stage set.

But — and this is critical — Memphis discipline is not chaos. Sottsass knew exactly what he was doing. The clashes are intentional. The geometry is knowingly artificial. Serious systems can be sabotaged by delight, but they have to be serious first.

---

## Typography

- **Display:** Bricolage Grotesque — 800 weight, aggressive scale shifts
- **Body:** DM Sans — clean, readable, contrasts with the wild display
- **Mono:** DM Mono — for metadata, used sparingly as a counter-voice
- **Mixed families:** This issue is the ONE issue where mixing is intentional. Display aggressive, body restrained.
- **Oversized punctuation:** Large quotation marks as visual devices, giant exclamation points as section markers

---

## Colour

Background: cream (#FAFAF0). Accent: orange (#FF6B35). Secondary: shocking pink (#FF1493). Third: yellow (#FFD700).

The colour clashes are structural: an orange section opener followed immediately by a shocking-pink quote-only spread is the point. The reader should feel the shift.

Patterns are used as backgrounds: diagonal hatching, repeating dots, chevron fields — always in transparent versions of the accent colours.

---

## Memphis Pattern Language

Every third section opener gets a pattern background. Options:
- Repeating diagonal lines (2px, 30° angle) in the accent colour at 8% opacity
- Concentric circles in the secondary colour at 6% opacity
- Checkered field in black at 4% opacity

These should feel like Memphis laminates — bold but measured.

## Spreads to Include

1. **Cover** — Memphis pattern background. Large stacked display type. No photography.
2. **Pattern Field** — A full-viewport spread that is nothing but pattern and one statement
3. **Editor's Note** — The note should open with a provocation, not an explanation
4. **Contents** — Bold colour blocks for each section entry
5. **Feature** — One strong image + aggressive typographic treatment
6. **Color Interlude** — Full orange spread with white type. Should feel joyful, not alarming.
7. **Texture Interlude** — Pattern as rest note between dense sections
8. **Section openers** — Each one different. Memphis refuses template sameness.
9. **Back matter** — Stamp overlay. Seal as a Memphis badge.

---

## Voice Notes

This issue can be playful in its language. Sentences can be short. Punchy. Headlines can break grammar conventions if the break is the point. Punctuation can be theatrical.

"The scene had an address." can be a whole paragraph. That's Memphis: compression, declaration, wit.

---

## The Memphis Test

Is the colour clash intentional? Can you defend *why* these colours clash here?  
Is the pattern structural content, or is it just decoration pretending to be a statement?  
Does the spread feel like a stage set? Does it know it's performing?  

If the answer to all three is yes, you're in Memphis territory.
